Questions

Before you book

Can this damage my TV?

No. We don't open the TV or change its software underneath. Apps are switched off, never deleted, and the picture, sound and network settings stay as they are.

What if we don't like it?

One switch in the settings brings back the TV's original home screen, exactly as it was. We'll show you where it is.

Will Netflix, YouTube and my other apps still work?

Yes. They're all still there, with your accounts signed in, and the remote's buttons still open them.

Is there a monthly charge?

No. You pay once. Internet is only needed for things like weather and the cricket score; the games work without it.

Does it work without internet or during load-shedding?

The TV works as normal when it's back on. The games and the clock work offline, and the weather and score update once the internet returns.
